Error 1: Fixing the update_email method for the Client Class.
    My initial method to update client' emails consisted of defining a new_email parameter, to pass the client's new email address into the method, and then using a print statement to confirm the change as seen below.

    Evidence(Code before the Change)
    def update_email (self,new_email):
        print(f"Your email has updated to: {new_email}")

    But what I realised was that the new email address wasn't actually being assigned to the email attribute of Client object. To resolve this I firstly assigned the new_email value to self.email to actually change the email stored within that particular Client object.

    Code after Implementing the Change

        #Assigning the new email value to self.email to store the new email for that specific Client object.
        self.email = new_email
